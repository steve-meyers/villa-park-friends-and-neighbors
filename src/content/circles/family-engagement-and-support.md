This is about supporting families in Villa Park! Whether that is advocating for family-friendly improvements to the neighborhood or planning events together, this is the go-to spot for families. Want to get involved and help make it easier to raise a family in VP? Reach out on the [Contact](/contact) page!

### Current Projects & Wins

We recently had a huge win with the installation of an accessible porta potty in Martinez Park! This was one of the very first projects we began in the Family Engagement and Support Circle, and we couldn't be prouder that, as a result of our advocacy, Martinez Park now has a bathroom for all park-goers to enjoy. \
Read more in this blog post: [Advocacy Win: Porta Potties for the People | Villa Park Friends and Neighbors, Denver](https://villaparkfans.com/blog/advocacy-win-porta-potties-for-the-people/)
