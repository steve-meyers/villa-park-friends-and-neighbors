This is about supporting families in Villa Park! Whether that is advocating for family-friendly improvements to the neighborhood or planning events together, this is the go-to spot for families. Want to get involved and help make it easier to raise a family in VP? Reach out on the [Contact](/contact) page!

### Current Projects

Have you noticed that the new Joe P. Martinez Park does not have a bathroom? Well, many of us have noticed, and it's particularly an issue for families trying to spend time at the playground with their kids. To fix this, we are currently in talks with Denver Parks and Rec about installing a porta potty on site as part of a "pilot project" with the city. We hope that this will be installed sometime later in 2026!

Update: We recently spotted a porta potty in Martinez Park. We're still waiting to confirm that this is a "permanent" install, but it's great progress!
