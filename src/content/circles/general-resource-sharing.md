## Resources

#### Colorado Poverty Law Project:

CPLP offers **FREE** Benefits Assistance Services to help individuals and families with disabilities access public benefits that can support financial stability, health, and housing stability.

**Their Benefits Assistance Program provides individualized support, including:**

* Benefits counseling to help determine which programs you may qualify for
* Application assistance to help you complete and submit benefits applications
* Documentation support to help gather medical, financial, and other required documents
* Guidance throughout the application process and help understanding next steps
* Referrals to community resources and legal services when additional support is needed
* Legal advocacy and appeals for eligible cases

##### What Benefits Can They Help With?

Their team can provide assistance with applications, questions, and other issues related to:

* Supplemental Security Income (SSI)
* Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI)
* Medicaid
* Division of Vocational Rehabilitation (DVR) services
* Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)
* Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF)
* Other federal, state, and local public assistance programs, depending on your circumstances

**All services are provided free of charge.** 

Follow this link to learn more and get legal help: [Benefits Assistance Program - Colorado Poverty Law Project](https://copovertylawproject.org/our-work/benefits-assistance-program/)
