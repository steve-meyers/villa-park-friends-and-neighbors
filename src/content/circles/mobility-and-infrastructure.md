This circle is about sidewalks, streets, paths, and all things getting around VP! Please join as we take action to make this neighborhood a more accessible and easy place to get around. 

Have other ideas for this circle? Reach out on the Contact page.

### Current Projects

We are currently in talks with the ownership at Villa Park Liquors and the Villa Park Mini Mart about installing a bike rack at their businesses. This would make it easier and safer to ride to these local destinations, and hopefully improve their sales as well!
