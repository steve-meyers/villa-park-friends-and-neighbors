Denver Parks & Recreation, Councilwoman Jamie Torres, and Villa Park neighbors celebrated the completed first phase of Joe P. Martinez Park's revitalization — a new playground, cultural play area, sports court, memorial plaza, and pavilion/amphitheater. The park is the only one located entirely within Villa Park.
