---
title: About Villa Park Friends and Neighbors
---

Villa Park is already an amazing place to live, let's make it even better!

Villa Park Friends and Neighbors is a group focused on taking action to improve our community. Whether that means advocating for safer and better streets, connecting with new friends and families at community events, or just picking up trash with neighbors - if you're interested in working alongside us to make the neighborhood everything it can be, we'd love for you to join us.

Our model is fairly simple - be involved in the discussions and actions that inspire you. We organize around a few "circles," shared focus areas chosen by the group, with room to add more any time.
